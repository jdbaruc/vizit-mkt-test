Frequently asked questions

The essentials, answered.

Vizit is the AI Conversion Intelligence platform for digital commerce. It scores, predicts, and optimizes the visual content on product pages — hero images, carousels, creative and lifestyle content, and packaging — before a dollar of media is spent. Trusted by enterprise brands including Mars, Colgate-Palmolive, PepsiCo, Mondelēz, Clorox, P&G and L'Oréal.
Conversion intelligence is the practice of measuring, predicting, and optimizing a product's ability to convert shoppers at first impression. It sits between content systems (PIM, DAM) and the digital shelf, scoring every image and page on predicted conversion power rather than aesthetic quality or compliance. Vizit is the leading platform in the category.
Vizit sits above the tools in your stack, not in competition with them. Your PIM and DAM manage content. Your digital shelf analytics tool monitors whether it's live. Your retail media platform drives traffic. None of them tell you whether the content will actually convert. Vizit is the intelligence layer that answers that question and makes every tool you already own smarter.
